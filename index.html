Why This Reads Better

  • Sections are grouped by how your energy changes.

    Morning prep, office sprints, recovery, and evening deep work are clearly separated.

  • Study types have distinct colors without making the screen noisy.

    Go and communication are instantly distinguishable, while breaks stay subdued.

  • Each block tells you what the session is for.

    You do not have to remember whether a slot is for drilling, review, or wrap-up.

Execution Rules

  • Use the `10:00-11:00 AM` slot for the hardest Go problems. Protect that hour first.

  • If office interruptions kill one Go block, compress review work before cutting the `9:00 PM` communication session.

  • End the day at `11:00 PM` consistently. The timetable depends on sleep more than extra late study.
